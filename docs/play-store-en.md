## Full description

SorGez plans your next trip in a few taps. Type where you are going, answer a few short questions, and get a day-by-day, hour-by-hour trip plan.

FIVE QUICK QUESTIONS
• Where: cities and countries are suggested as you type, and the globe turns to your pick.
• How long and when: from a weekend to two weeks, and the month you travel.
• Who with: solo, with a partner, with family or with friends.
• Budget: budget, mid-range or luxury.
• Interests: history, food, nature, museums, shopping, nightlife, beaches, adventure.

A PLAN FOR EVERY DAY
• Each day on its own page, with a timed timeline.
• A short note and an estimated cost for every stop.
• Suggestions that suit the season of your month, plus practical tips.
• Share the plan with friends in one tap.

SIMPLE AND FAST
• No account needed.
• 15 languages and four currencies (TRY, EUR, USD, GBP).

Plans are written by AI; please check times, prices and opening days before you go.
